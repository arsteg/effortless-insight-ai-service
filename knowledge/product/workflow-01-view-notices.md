To see your GST notices:
1. Web: open Notices from the sidebar (/notices). Mobile: Notices tab.
2. Filter by status (uploaded/processing/analyzed/in progress/responded/closed), priority, GSTIN, free-text search, or deadline window.
3. Quick views on web: /notices?overdue=true for overdue, /notices?dueWithinDays=7 for due this week, /notices?gstin=XX for one GSTIN.
4. Tap/click a notice to open its detail page with all tabs.

The dashboard (web /dashboard, mobile Home) shows counts of active, due-soon, and overdue notices plus upcoming deadlines.

The assistant can list notices directly when asked (for example "show my high priority notices" or "which notices are overdue"), then offer to open the filtered list.
