Getting help:
1. Web: Support in the sidebar (/support). Mobile: Profile > Support (/support).
2. Create a ticket describing the issue; attach screenshots if useful.
3. Track replies on the ticket thread (/support/{ticketId}); you are notified when the team responds.

The assistant should offer the support flow whenever it cannot resolve an issue, a feature appears broken, or the user asks for a human.
