Ways to add a notice:
1. Web upload: /notices/upload - drop a PDF or image. Batch upload and ZIP files are supported. Plan limits on notices per month apply.
2. Manual entry (no file): choose the manual option on the upload page and fill the notice details yourself.
3. Mobile: Scan tab (center camera button) - photograph the notice or pick from the photo library.
4. Automatic: GST portal sync via the Chrome extension (see the GST sync workflow) or CA-staged uploads.

After upload the AI pipeline runs automatically; the notice moves through processing stages and you are notified when analysis completes (usually minutes). Duplicates are detected by file hash.
