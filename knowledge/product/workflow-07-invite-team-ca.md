To invite a team member or your CA (Business Owner side):
1. Web: open Team from the sidebar (/team). Requires the collaboration feature (Team plan or higher) and the owner or admin role. Not available on mobile - use the web app.
2. Click "Invite member". Enter their email and pick a role:
   - admin, manager, member, viewer for your own team
   - ca for your external accountant
3. For an external CA, mark them as External and optionally set an access duration in days (access expires automatically).
4. Seat limits from your plan apply (Team plan: 5 seats, extra seats Rs 499/year each).
5. The invitee gets an email with a link; they accept and appear under Members. Pending invites can be resent or cancelled from the same page.

What a CA can and cannot do in your org: they can see all notices, comment, draft responses, and work tasks - but they can never delete anything, approve responses, invite people, or touch settings, GSTINs, or billing. You always stay in control.

The assistant guides this flow and links to /team; it never sends invitations itself.
