EffortlessInsight is an AI-powered GST notice management SaaS for Indian businesses.

What it does:
- Ingests GST notices four ways: file upload (PDF/images, batch/ZIP), manual entry, automatic GST-portal sync via a Chrome extension, and CA-staged uploads for clients.
- Runs an AI pipeline on every notice: OCR, data extraction, classification, risk scoring (0-100, low/medium/high/critical), and a plain-language report in English and Hindi with action items, required documents, and legal references.
- Tracks response deadlines with automatic reminders (7/3/1/0 days before, at 09:00 IST, to the assignee) plus missed-deadline alerts, daily digests, and weekly summaries.
- Supports collaboration: team members and external CAs can comment, create tasks, request documents, and draft responses with a review/approval workflow.
- Drafts AI-generated replies to notices (tone and language selectable), which a human reviews, approves, and files on the government portal themselves.

What it does NOT do:
- It never files or submits anything to the government GST portal (gst.gov.in). Final filing is always a human action outside the app.
- It does not provide legal advice. AI output is assistance only and carries a disclaimer; users should consult their CA for decisions with legal or financial consequences.

Platforms: web app (app.effortlessinsight.in), mobile app (Android/iOS), WhatsApp command bot, and a Chrome extension for GST portal sync.
