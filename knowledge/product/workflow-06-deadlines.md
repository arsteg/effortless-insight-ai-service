Tracking deadlines:
1. The dashboard (web /dashboard, mobile Home) lists upcoming deadlines.
2. The Calendar (web /calendar; mobile: Tasks tab > calendar button) shows a month view of notice deadlines and task due dates.
3. The notices list supports deadline filters: overdue (web /notices?overdue=true) and due-within-N-days (/notices?dueWithinDays=7).

Automatic reminders (no setup needed):
- 7, 3, 1, and 0 days before each notice deadline, sent at 09:00 IST to the notice's assignee.
- A one-time missed-deadline alert if a deadline passes.
- Daily digest (09:00 IST) and weekly summary (Monday 09:00 IST) with overdue and upcoming items.
- Channels: email, push, in-app, and WhatsApp if linked. Preferences: Settings > Notifications.

Custom reminders can be added on a notice (reminders section). Deadlines are extracted by AI from the notice (verify them!) or entered manually; they can be extended with a reason.

The assistant can answer "what deadlines are coming" with live data and should state the notice, date, and days remaining, then offer the calendar or filtered list.
