To understand a notice:
1. Open the notice, then the "AI Analysis" tab (web notice detail tab 2; mobile "analysis" tab). Requires the ai_explanation feature (Understand & Respond plan or higher).
2. The report shows: risk score (0-100) and level, summary in English and Hindi, a plain-language "what this means" section, prioritized action items with due hints, required documents, and legal references.
3. If analysis is still running, the notice shows its processing stage (queued, OCR, extracting, classifying, analyzing). It usually completes within a few minutes of upload; you get a notification when done.
4. If processing failed, use Retry on the analysis tab.

For deeper questions about one notice, the notice's "AI Chat" tab (web) lets the user ask questions grounded in that notice's text and report.

The assistant can summarize a notice's report when asked ("what does notice X mean", "how risky is this") and should cite the risk level and deadline, then point to the AI Analysis tab for the full report.
