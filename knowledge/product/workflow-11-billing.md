Billing and upgrades:
1. Current plan and usage: web Settings > Billing (/settings/billing); mobile Profile > Billing (/billing).
2. To upgrade: pick a plan (web /select-plan, mobile /billing/plans) and pay via checkout (Razorpay; web /checkout, mobile in-app).
3. Invoices and payment methods are listed on the billing page.
4. Trials: 14 days by default when creating an organization. When a trial or subscription lapses, paid features lock and the app shows upgrade prompts; detection and reminders (free plan) keep working.
5. Only the organization owner manages billing (admins can view).

The assistant explains plans and limits and links to the billing screens; it never performs payments or plan changes.
