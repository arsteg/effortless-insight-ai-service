WhatsApp assistant (requires the whatsapp_assistant feature - Understand & Respond plan or higher):
1. Web: Settings > WhatsApp (/settings/whatsapp). Link your WhatsApp number with an OTP verification.
2. Once linked, message the EffortlessInsight WhatsApp number with commands:
   - status - account and notice overview
   - notices - recent notices
   - deadlines - upcoming deadlines
   - tasks - your open tasks
   - help - list commands
   - stop - pause messages (opt out)
3. High-risk notice alerts and deadline reminders can also arrive on WhatsApp based on your notification preferences.
4. Unlink any time from the same settings page.
