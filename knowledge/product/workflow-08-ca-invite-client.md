For CA accounts (CA-as-distributor) - inviting and managing clients:
1. Web: open the client workspace at /clients/invite (also reachable from the "Invite Client" button on the dashboard). CA accounts only.
2. Add a client by GSTIN + email and send the invitation, or "Save client without inviting" to create a prospect first.
3. You can upload notices for a prospect before they join - they stage in your CA workspace and transfer automatically when the client accepts.
4. The client receives an email link (valid 14 days, up to 3 resends - each resend invalidates the old link). On acceptance their organization is created (or linked if it already exists with that GSTIN), staged notices move over, and you join their org as their CA.
5. The client's organization needs an active or trial subscription to complete acceptance.
6. Your clients list shows staged prospects and active clients with notice and overdue counts. Switch into a client's workspace via the client selector in the header.

The assistant guides this flow and links to /clients/invite; it never creates or sends client invitations itself.
