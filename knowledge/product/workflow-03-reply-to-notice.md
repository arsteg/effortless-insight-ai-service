To reply to a notice:
1. Open the notice, go to the "Response" tab.
2. Write the reply yourself, or click Auto-Draft (requires the draft_reply feature). Auto-draft options: tone (formal / conciliatory / defensive) and language (English / Hindi). AI drafts are a starting point and always carry a disclaimer - a human must review every word.
3. Save Draft as you work.
4. Submit for Review when ready. An owner, admin, or manager then approves or rejects it (rejection returns it to draft with comments).
5. After approval, a human files the response on the GST portal (gst.gov.in) - the app NEVER files for you - and then marks it as Submitted in the app.
6. Supporting documents (PDF/JPG/PNG up to 10 MB) can be attached as response documents.

Response status flow: draft -> review -> approved -> submitted.

The assistant may offer to trigger an auto-draft (with tone/language choices) but only via a confirmation card; approval and marking-as-submitted are human-only actions the assistant never performs.
