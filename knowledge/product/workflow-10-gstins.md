Managing GSTINs:
1. Organization GSTINs: web Settings > Organization (/settings/organization). Owner or admin only. Add or remove GSTINs and set the primary one. GSTINs are validated on entry and plan limits apply (free and Understand & Respond: 1 GSTIN; Team: 10; Enterprise: unlimited).
2. GST-sync monitoring is separate: /gst-sync > Connected GSTINs controls which GSTINs the Chrome extension watches for new portal notices.
3. GSTN portal connection (OTP-based, per GSTIN) lives under Settings > Integrations on both web and mobile.

Not available on mobile - organization GSTIN management is web-only.

If the user hits a GSTIN limit, explain the plan limit and point to Settings > Billing to upgrade.
